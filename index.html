About us

An independent audio engineering company

ACD AUDIO LTD, trading as ACD Audio, is an audio engineering company based in Farnborough, Hampshire. The company was incorporated on 24 March 2023 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 14755732.

Our work is focused on audio and sound systems: planning and setting up systems for the space and the job, tuning and configuring equipment, and giving technical support when something isn't working as it should.

We are a small, director-led company with no parent group, so clients have a direct technical point of contact for their audio work.

Services

Audio and sound system engineering, and technical support

A short overview of the kind of work we do. Every job is different, so the details are agreed with each client.

Sound system engineering

Planning how an audio system should be put together for a particular space and purpose, then setting it up and checking it works as intended.

Setup and tuning

Configuring audio equipment and adjusting system settings so that a sound system sounds right in the room and runs reliably.

Technical support

Practical advice on audio equipment and signal flow, and help tracing and fixing faults in existing sound systems.
